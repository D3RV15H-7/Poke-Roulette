      ℹ About PokéRoulette

      PokéRoulette lets you spin for a Pokémon or search by name or Pokédex number, then explore its evolutions and trading cards.

      Your Collection

      Mark Pokémon and card variations as collected in the Dex and Sets views. Use Profile to sign in and manage account sync. Your collection is also saved on this device.

      Data & Media Sources
      • Pokémon names, types, evolutions and artwork: PokéAPI
      • Card sets, images and variations: Pokémon TCG API and TCGdex
      • Card values, where available, are estimates based on provider market data and shown in GBP. They may differ from sale prices.
